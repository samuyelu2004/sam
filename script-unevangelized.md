# The Village of No Name — HD-2D HyperFrames Explanation
**Question:** What happens to people who never heard of Jesus? If Jesus is the only way to the Father, what about a man born in a remote village who died without ever hearing his name?

**Voice:** voice-01

## SCENE 1 — THE QUESTION (ACT I)
What happens to people who never heard of Jesus? If Jesus is the only way to the Father, what about a man born in a remote village who died without ever hearing his name? People ask this in our comments all the time, so let's allow the Bible to answer it.

## SCENE 2 — CORNELIUS IN CAESAREA (ACT II)
The Bible's clearest window into this is a man named Cornelius. He was a Roman centurion in Caesarea — a Gentile, not a Jew — yet he feared God, gave generously to the poor, and prayed continually.

## SCENE 3 — THE VISITOR (ACT III)
One afternoon, an angel appeared to him with a command: send to Joppa, and bring a man named Simon Peter. When Peter arrived and preached the gospel, the Holy Spirit fell on Cornelius and his entire household — before a single Jewish ritual was required. The first Gentile converts, reached exactly where they stood.

## SCENE 4 — ANSWER ONE: NOT A RESPECTER OF PERSONS (ACT IV)
Then Peter said something extraordinary. God is no respecter of persons. But in every nation, he that fears him and does what is right is accepted with him. That is answer one. God is not waiting for perfect knowledge. He moves toward anyone reaching for him, even through the faintest light.

## SCENE 5 — ANSWER TWO: NO ONE WITHOUT LIGHT (ACT V)
Answer two: no one is truly without light. Paul says the eternal power of God is visible in what he has made — every sunrise declares it. He writes that the law is written on every human heart. And in Athens, standing before a pillar inscribed to the unknown God, Paul said the God they were already groping after is the God he had been sent to declare.

## SCENE 6 — ANSWER THREE: JUDGED FAIRLY BY THE LIGHT GIVEN (ACT VI)
Which brings answer three: God judges fairly, by the light each person was given. To whomsoever much is given, of him shall much be required. And if a city as broken as Sodom could be spared for the sake of ten righteous, how much more does the judge of all the earth do right?

## SCENE 7 — WHERE CHRISTIANS DISAGREE (ACT VII)
Christians still disagree on how this plays out — whether the gospel must be consciously heard and trusted in this life, or whether Christ can save those who never learned his name. But every view agrees on one thing: there is no salvation outside of Jesus. No other name is given under heaven.

## SCENE 8 — THE WEIGHT ON OUR SHOULDERS (ACT VIII)
Then Paul asks a question that puts the weight back on our shoulders. How shall they call on him in whom they have not believed? And how shall they believe in him of whom they have not heard? And how shall they hear without a preacher? The answer to the village of no name is us.
