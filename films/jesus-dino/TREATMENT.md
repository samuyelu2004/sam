# Jesus & the Dinosaur — 15-second pixel-art micro-story

A whimsical sunset-boat conversation about human evolution, adaptation, and kindness. Jesus and a friendly green dinosaur share a small wooden ship; rain arrives mid-conversation.

- 1920×1080, 24fps, 16-bit-inspired pixel art (320×180 framebuffer, nearest-neighbour upscale)
- New composition about every two seconds, with brief pixel-wipe transitions
- RPG-style dialogue captions; original chiptune, sea ambience, rain and distant thunder
- Dialogue is captioned, not voiced
